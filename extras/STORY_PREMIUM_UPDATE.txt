used loaded local content with a storage stub. Real PWA installation, actual
cache operation, physical-device speech and mobile testing are not verified.
This is a development build, not a publishable paid app. Professional recorded
narration, distinct illustrations for each scene, complete Italian/other
translations, two missing animal calls (polar bear and fennec fox), authenticity
and commercial rights review of the 21 existing animal recordings, and real
Google Play Billing remain outstanding. The €9.99 permanent pass is a proposed
price, not evidence of demand. The app remains ad-free and subscription-free.
