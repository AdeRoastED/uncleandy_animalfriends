Hello, my wonderful young explorers, and welcome!

I'm Uncle Andy, and just like you, I absolutely love discovering the amazing secrets of our planet! For over four decades, I've been fascinated by the magic of nature. Seeing green forests calms my heart, and every animal, big or small, fills my mind with wonder and curiosity!

I've been lucky enough to travel to incredible places, from the sunny lands of Mexico and the beautiful countries of Europe, to the bustling wilds of Asia, where I lived for over fifteen years. I'm even a teacher! But my biggest wish was always to share the incredible stories of the natural world with curious kids just like you.

So, I wrote these books! I hope you'll enjoy reading these adventures and learning about amazing creatures as much as I loved exploring and researching them. Get ready to imagine every leap, every sound, and every secret hiding place!

Now, let the adventure begin, my young explorers!
