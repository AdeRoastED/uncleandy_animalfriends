Mimi's Morning

Mimi opened her eyes in a square of golden sunshine. She stretched one paw, then another, until she looked almost twice as long as an ordinary cat. “A very successful morning,” she purred, although she had done absolutely nothing yet. From the other side of the room came a little jingle. A kitten was batting a soft toy with a bell sewn securely inside it.

Morning · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/cat-page-03.mp3
Mimi's Morning

The kitten gave the toy one enthusiastic whack. It rolled under the big armchair. Jingle, bump, silence! Mimi peered underneath. She could see a little corner of the toy beside the chair leg. “How rude of you to hide,” she told it.

Mimi took one more look. The smell of the toy was still on the rug. Mimi followed it with her nose until she reached the edge of the armchair, where a rather dusty fluff ball was pretending to be something interesting. She gave it an experimental pat. It stuck to her paw. “An entirely useless clue,” she muttered, shaking it away. The kitten watched from behind her, ears pricked. Then her ears pricked at the sound of bowls rattling in the kitchen.

Her human called the cats for breakfast, and Mimi decided that a proper detective should never investigate on an empty stomach.

Morning · page 3

Mimi's Morning

After breakfast, Mimi washed her face with a damp paw. The kitten chased a patch of sunlight across the floor, forgetting the missing toy for a while. Mimi noticed that the armchair had a wider opening at the back than at the front. She sniffed it, flicked her whiskers and climbed onto the windowsill. A warm nap seemed much more urgent than detective work.

Afternoon · page 1
Target: audio/bedtime/cat-page-05.mp3
Mimi's Afternoon

In the afternoon the kitten began searching everywhere. It looked in its basket, beside the curtains and even underneath Mimi's tail. “Excuse me!” Mimi protested. The kitten gave a miserable little meow. Its favourite toy was missing. Mimi remembered the morning's jingle and led the kitten towards the armchair.

The kitten had tried to entertain itself with a crumpled piece of paper, but the paper did not jingle. It had tried a ball, but the ball did not feel quite right. Mimi suddenly remembered the first toy she had ever loved: a little fabric mouse that had been almost as big as her head. She had carried it everywhere. She looked at the kitten again, and the mystery no longer seemed like an amusing way to pass the afternoon. It was somebody's very important treasure.

Mimi's Afternoon

She reached under the front with one paw. Nothing. She tried the other paw. Still nothing. Her whiskers brushed the narrow sides when she leaned closer. Cats use their sensitive whiskers to gather information about nearby objects and spaces. Mimi decided not to squeeze herself into the gap. “A detective must keep her whiskers in excellent condition,” she announced.

Afternoon · page 3
Target: audio/bedtime/cat-page-07.mp3
Mimi's Afternoon

Then she remembered the opening at the back. She walked around the chair, crouched and reached in from the wider side. Her paw touched something soft. Jingle! The kitten bounced with excitement. Mimi hooked the edge of the toy and pulled it slowly into the light. But just as she did, she discovered how wonderfully it rolled across the floor.

The bell made a cheerful sound, and Mimi's paws seemed to have minds of their own. Before she knew it, she had chased the toy halfway across the rug and stopped it neatly between her front feet. What a splendid toy! The kitten followed, then sat at a polite little distance, with its tail curled around its paws. Mimi looked down at the prize. She had done all the work of finding it. But the kitten had been waiting all day for this moment, too.

Evening · page 1

Mimi's Evening

Mimi gave the toy a gentle tap. Jingle! She tapped it again. The kitten watched, waiting. Mimi could have played with it all evening, but she remembered the kitten's sad little face. She nudged the toy towards its owner instead. “Your turn, little detective.” The kitten pounced, rolled over and landed with all four paws in the air.

Evening · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/cat-page-09.mp3
Mimi's Evening

Their human joined them for a little supervised play, rolling the toy across the rug. Mimi discovered that watching the kitten's ridiculous leaps was almost as entertaining as chasing the toy herself.

For the first time that afternoon, the kitten was so busy playing that it forgot to look sad. It pounced on the empty rug, missed the toy completely and gave an offended little meow. Mimi rolled onto her side, unable to resist joining in. Their human made sure the game stayed gentle, and soon two shadows were bouncing across the carpet. The kitten was purring even as it waited for the next turn.

When the game ended, their human put the toy away for another day. The kitten climbed into its basket, then changed its mind and snuggled beside Mimi.

Mimi's Evening

Outside the window, the last sunlight disappeared. Mimi listened to the kitten's tiny purr, which sounded rather like a miniature motor. She had found something more enjoyable than keeping a treasure all to herself: making someone else happy. She tucked her paws beneath her chest, and the two cats drifted off like two warm little loaves of bread.
