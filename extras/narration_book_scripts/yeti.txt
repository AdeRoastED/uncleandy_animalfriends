Yeti's Morning — 1

In our imaginary adventure high in the Himalayan mountains, Yeti stepped out of his cave and inspected the fresh snow. The Himalayas really do contain some of the world's highest mountains, but Yeti is a creature of legend. In this story, his feet were so enormous that a single footprint looked like a little bathtub.

Morning · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/yeti-page-03.mp3
Yeti's Morning — 2

He decided to make a snow face beside a great boulder. Two pebbles became eyes, and a bent twig became a smile. “Much better looking than me,” he declared. Behind the boulder was a sheltered cave, which Yeti knew well. The wind hardly reached inside, and the floor was dry and comfortable.

Yeti crouched beside the snow face and adjusted its twig smile. From behind the boulder, the wind was much softer, and the little cave felt pleasantly sheltered. He imagined himself running a mountain museum of funny snow faces. The first exhibit would have a nose made of snow; the second would have a spectacular moustache. He studied the large boulder carefully, because its shape was easy to recognise among the smaller rocks. Even on a snowy mountain in a magical story, it was useful to know where a comfortable shelter could be found.

Yeti's Morning — 3

An imaginary little mountain spirit named Pema arrived, laughing at the snow face. Together they made another one, much smaller, with a ridiculous snow moustache. Then they followed their familiar path, staying near Yeti's home. Pema liked collecting funny stories, and Yeti liked telling them in his deep, rumbling voice.

Afternoon · page 1
Target: audio/bedtime/yeti-page-05.mp3
Yeti's Afternoon — 1

After midday, clouds drifted over the mountain. Snow began falling more heavily, and Pema could no longer see the distant rocks. The little spirit looked worried.

The distant peaks disappeared behind the falling snow. Pema looked towards the place where the little spirit's family usually appeared, but the familiar shapes were no longer visible. Yeti stopped making jokes for a moment. He knew Pema was not enjoying the game anymore. Instead of showing off how quickly his enormous feet could carry him, he looked for the nearby boulder. Its dark shape was still visible through the falling flakes. The little snow faces were probably being covered, but the sheltered cave behind them would be a much better place to wait.

Yeti stopped their game. He remembered the boulder and its sheltered cave, and led Pema back along the short, familiar path towards their morning snow faces.

Yeti's Afternoon — 2

The little moustache had almost disappeared beneath the new snow, but the huge boulder was still unmistakable. Inside the cave, the wind became a quiet whistle. Pema shivered. Yeti offered a soft bundle of imaginary mountain wool and sat nearby, leaving the little spirit plenty of comfortable space.

Afternoon · page 3
Target: audio/bedtime/yeti-page-07.mp3
Yeti's Afternoon — 3

“Would you like a story?” he asked. Pema nodded. Yeti invented a tale about a mountain goat who wanted to become a snowflake and kept landing on his own nose. Pema laughed, and the two watched the snowfall from inside the shelter instead of trying to travel through it.

Yeti made the imaginary mountain goat's story even sillier. Every time the goat tried to become a snowflake, it landed in a different funny position, until Pema was laughing so hard that the little spirit forgot to be frightened for a moment. Outside the cave, the snow still fell, and they remained comfortably sheltered. Yeti listened when Pema wanted to talk and told another little joke when Pema wanted to laugh. The enormous mountain looked much friendlier from a safe place with somebody kind beside you.

Yeti's Evening — 1

Towards evening the snowfall eased, and the familiar path became visible again. In their magical mountain world, Pema's family appeared near the boulder to take the little spirit home. Yeti waved from the shelter. Pema gave him a tiny snowball shaped like a heart as a thank-you present.

Evening · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/yeti-page-09.mp3
Yeti's Evening — 2

Yeti looked at his enormous hands and feet. He had always thought those were the most impressive things about him. But the little spirit had needed a safe place, a patient friend and a funny story, not a giant display of strength. He placed the snowball beside his cave entrance.

Yeti admired the tiny heart-shaped snowball. It was not as large as his thumb, but he felt it was the finest treasure on the mountain. He looked towards the two nearly buried snow faces and decided to rebuild them on another clear day. Today he had discovered a new kind of adventure, one in which nobody needed to climb the highest peak or prove how strong they were. A warm shelter, a funny story and a little patience had made a very good afternoon. He listened to the mountain grow quiet.

Yeti's Evening — 3

The stars appeared over the snowy peaks. Yeti settled inside his cave and listened to the soft mountain wind. Being big could be useful, but being gentle was something even the smallest creature could practise. He smiled, closed his eyes and dreamed of two snow faces wearing the world's silliest moustaches.
