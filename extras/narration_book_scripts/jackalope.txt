Jackie's Morning — 1

In the wide North American prairie of our make-believe adventure, Jackie the Jackalope bounced out of his cosy home. A jackalope is a legendary rabbit with antlers, not a real species. Jackie was especially proud of his imaginary antlers, although they had one annoying habit: they seemed to collect absolutely everything.

Morning · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/jackalope-page-03.mp3
Jackie's Morning — 2

A dry leaf caught on one of the antlers. “A fashionable hat!” Jackie exclaimed. He hopped past a broad bush that cast a comfortable patch of shade over the ground. Beneath it, the air felt cooler. He paused for a moment, admired his leafy hat and memorised the little shady spot.

Jackie sat beneath the bush for a moment and admired the leaf balanced on his imaginary antlers. The shade was wonderfully comfortable, even though the morning was still fairly cool. He could hear the prairie grass whispering in the breeze and see tiny insects moving between the stems. “This is the Royal Resting Place of the Grand Wiggly Carrot,” he announced to nobody in particular. Then he bounced away, eager to find Juniper. He had no idea how useful a resting place might become in the middle of his favourite race.

Jackie's Morning — 3

His imaginary rabbit friend Juniper arrived, ready for their favourite hopping game. They took turns bouncing between familiar patches of grass. Jackie usually won because he was so enthusiastic, although Juniper was much better at choosing funny names for the finishing lines. Today's finish was called the Grand Wiggly Carrot.

Afternoon · page 1
Target: audio/bedtime/jackalope-page-05.mp3
Jackie's Afternoon — 1

By afternoon, the sun was much hotter. Jackie was ready for one more race, but Juniper began to slow down. “My paws feel tired,” she admitted. Jackie had almost reached the Grand Wiggly Carrot and wanted to finish first. Then he looked back and noticed that his friend was no longer smiling.

The finishing line was only a few hops away. Jackie could almost imagine the imaginary cheering crowd shouting his name. He glanced behind him, expecting Juniper to be laughing, but she had stopped. Her ears drooped a little, and the grass around them was no longer moving in a cool breeze. Jackie looked at the finish line again. Winning would take only a moment, but Juniper did not seem to be having fun anymore. He suddenly remembered the shady bush and his silly leafy hat. Perhaps the best part of the game was not the finish line after all.

Jackie's Afternoon — 2

He stopped the game immediately. “The carrot can wait,” he said. He remembered the broad bush from the morning and suggested resting in the shade. Juniper agreed, and they made their way to the familiar spot at a comfortable pace. Jackie sat beside her and gave her room to cool down.

Afternoon · page 3

Jackie's Afternoon — 3

They listened to the breeze moving through the grass. In their pretend prairie world, they had brought a little water flask for their outing, and Juniper took a refreshing drink. Jackie discovered another leaf on his antlers and presented it as a fancy parasol. Soon Juniper was laughing again.

Juniper took another comfortable sip and settled beneath the leaves. Jackie decided to make up a grand story about his very important antlers, which collected leaves because they were secretly trees. Juniper laughed and suggested that one of them might soon grow an apple. They stayed in the shade while the afternoon was hot, listening to the prairie sounds. There was no need to hurry back to the game. Jackie felt rather pleased when Juniper began smiling again, and he noticed that sitting together could be every bit as entertaining as racing.

Jackie's Evening — 1

When evening came and the air cooled, Juniper felt ready for a gentle game. This time Jackie asked what she wanted to play. They invented a slow-motion hopping contest, in which the funniest hop won instead of the fastest. Jackie performed such a dramatic wobble that both friends fell into fits of laughter.

Evening · page 2

Jackie's Evening — 2

At sunset they returned towards their homes together. “You could have won the race,” Juniper said. Jackie shook his antlers, sending the leafy hat fluttering away. “I think I won something better.” They had both enjoyed the day, and his friend hadn't needed to pretend she was comfortable when she wasn't.

They passed the Grand Wiggly Carrot on the way home, and Jackie gave the imaginary finish line a very grand bow. Juniper bowed too, then invented a new name for it: the Grand Wiggly Carrot That Could Wait. Jackie thought that was an excellent name. He no longer felt disappointed about the race they had stopped. They had found a better game and had enjoyed the evening together. Juniper picked up the leafy hat he had shaken loose and gave it back. Jackie wore it proudly for the rest of their walk.

Jackie's Evening — 3

Jackie tucked himself into his imaginary home and listened to the evening wind whispering across the prairie. A good friend notices when someone needs a break, he thought. He closed his eyes, dreaming of the Grand Wiggly Carrot and a whole collection of ridiculous leafy hats. Tomorrow, they could play again.
