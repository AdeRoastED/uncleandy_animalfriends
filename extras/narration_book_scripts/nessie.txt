Nessie's Morning — 1

Once upon a time, in a make-believe tale set on Scotland's famous Loch Ness, Nessie lifted her long neck above the mist. “Bloop!” she greeted the morning. Loch Ness is a real, deep freshwater lake, but Nessie belongs to the world of legends and imagination. Today, in our story, she had a very important festival to watch.

Morning · page 2

Nessie's Morning — 2

The little village beside the water was preparing a celebration. Paper lanterns decorated the windows, and an imaginary water sprite named Misty was arranging floating flowers near the shore. One small enchanted lantern drifted towards a sheltered inlet. Nessie gently guided it into the calm water beside a crooked willow.

The enchanted lantern drifted towards a crooked willow whose branches hung low above the water. The misty surface reflected the lantern's little glow. Nessie lowered her head to admire it. In our imaginary loch, the lantern could float safely without a flame, and its light looked like a tiny star that had decided to go swimming. The sheltered inlet was easy to recognise from the shape of the willow. Nessie hoped the little star would still be shining when the festival began.

Afternoon · page 1
Target: audio/bedtime/nessie-page-05.mp3
Nessie's Afternoon — 1

By afternoon, a thick mist had rolled across the loch. The houses disappeared from view, and the little sprite could no longer see the familiar shoreline. Misty called from the reeds. Nessie heard her worried voice and swam towards it, staying in the sheltered part of the water. “Here I am,” she answered gently.

The mist wrapped itself around the loch until even Nessie's long neck seemed to disappear halfway up. Misty called again, and Nessie answered with a soft bloop. The little sprite's light was still glowing, but the familiar shapes of the village were hidden. Nessie did not want to hurry into the middle of the loch where it would be harder to find their way. She remembered the blue ribbon and the crooked willow, and she listened for Misty's voice. Perhaps the little sheltered place they had discovered that morning would be the right place to wait.

Nessie's Afternoon — 2

Misty was frightened that she would miss the dance. Nessie remembered the crooked willow and the enchanted lantern from the morning. She followed the familiar bank towards the inlet while Misty fluttered beside her. Through the grey mist, a faint golden light appeared, and the blue ribbon moved in the breeze.

Afternoon · page 3

Nessie's Afternoon — 3

They reached the sheltered inlet and stayed there while the mist began to thin. Misty was still worried, so Nessie told her about the time she had mistaken a floating log for another monster. The sprite laughed so hard that her wings made little circles in the water. Waiting felt much less frightening together.

Misty laughed at Nessie's story about the log, and her lantern made a tiny reflection on the quiet water. As they waited, the thick mist began to turn into thinner wisps. First they could see the willow branches, then the blue ribbon, and finally a faint glow from one of the village windows. Nessie did not hurry the little sprite. The festival would be much more enjoyable if Misty arrived feeling comfortable rather than frightened. The enchanted lantern was still shining, as steadily as it had that morning.

Nessie's Evening — 1

When evening came, the village lights became visible again. Misty carried her magical lantern towards the festival, and Nessie watched from the quiet inlet. The sprite's dance began, and the light reflected in the water like a little golden star. Nobody needed to know who had helped her find it.

Evening · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/nessie-page-09.mp3
Nessie's Evening — 2

Misty returned to thank her friend. “Your light wasn't very big,” Nessie told her, “but when everything was grey, it was exactly what we needed.” The sprite gave Nessie a little bow, and Nessie replied with a graceful splash that was not quite as graceful as she had intended.

The little sprite held up a small star-shaped charm made in their magical village. Nessie admired it and watched the festival lights appear one by one across the water. Misty had not needed an enormous lantern to find a familiar place, and she had not needed to face the grey afternoon alone. Nessie liked the thought of being a quiet friend who could help someone wait until the world became clearer. She settled near the inlet, watching the tiny lights dance on the water like a whole sky of swimming stars.

Nessie's Evening — 3

Under the stars, Nessie slipped into the peaceful loch of our imagination. A small circle of ripples spread across the water and disappeared. The village lanterns glowed softly. Sometimes a little kindness can be a light for someone who feels lost, Nessie thought, before settling into her secret, storybook sleep.
