Bigfoot's Morning — 1

In a make-believe forest in North America, Bigfoot woke beneath the enormous trees and stretched his enormous toes. Bigfoot is a legendary creature, not a confirmed animal, but in our story he was a very real friend to the imaginary woodland folk. His morning footsteps went stomp, squelch, stomp through the damp soil.

Morning · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/bigfoot-page-03.mp3
Bigfoot's Morning — 2

Beside a little stream, a fallen branch stretched from one bank towards the other. Bigfoot noticed how narrow and wobbly it was. A little farther along, his familiar woodland path crossed a broad, flat patch of ground beside the stream. There was a solid wooden footbridge there, made by the imaginary forest folk.

Bigfoot paused at the sturdy bridge and looked at the carved leaf on its post. It was a little oak leaf, with a wiggly stem and tiny lines cut into the wood. The imaginary forest folk had chosen it so travellers could recognise the crossing. Bigfoot put one enormous hand on the railing. It was much more reassuring than the narrow branch near the stream. He had no trouble remembering the path to the bridge, and he hoped the smaller woodland creatures would remember it too.

Morning · page 3

Bigfoot's Morning — 3

A tiny woodland sprite named Fern appeared and admired the branch. “It looks like a balancing beam!” she exclaimed. Bigfoot chuckled. “It looks like a way to get very wet feet.” They continued their morning walk, and Bigfoot pointed out the little bridge, with its two sturdy railings and a carved leaf on the post.

Afternoon · page 1
Target: audio/bedtime/bigfoot-page-05.mp3
Bigfoot's Afternoon — 1

In the afternoon, rain fell over the forest. Water dripped from the branches, and the fallen limb became slippery. Fern returned to the stream, excited to visit her friend on the opposite bank. She thought the branch might be a quick way across, but stopped when she saw it wobble in the water.

The rain made the stream louder than it had been that morning. Fern watched the branch move against the bank and decided it did not look like such an excellent balancing beam after all. The sprite could see the other side of the stream, where a friend was waiting, but could not think of a safe way across. Fern called for Bigfoot among the dripping leaves, hoping he was nearby. There was no need to invent a dangerous shortcut when the forest folk had already made a proper bridge a little farther along the path.

Bigfoot's Afternoon — 2

Bigfoot heard her call and came along the path. He remembered the bridge with the carved leaf. “It takes a little longer,” he said, “but we don't have to turn ourselves into wet socks.” Fern laughed and followed him along the familiar path, well away from the slippery bank.

Afternoon · page 3
Target: audio/bedtime/bigfoot-page-07.mp3
Bigfoot's Afternoon — 3

The bridge was just as sturdy as it had been in the morning. Fern crossed with Bigfoot beside her, holding the railing in their imaginary woodland world. On the other side her friend was waiting. Bigfoot gave them both a friendly wave and began walking back beneath the dripping trees.

The familiar carved leaf beside the bridge shone with drops of rain. Below them, the stream hurried between the banks, carrying little leaves and twigs away. From the bridge, it was rather lovely to watch. Behind him, the sprite and her friend were already talking happily. Bigfoot did not need to make a great leap or lift the branch out of the water. He had simply remembered a safe place and helped someone find it.

Bigfoot's Evening — 1

At sunset, Fern and her friend brought him a little picture they had drawn on a leaf. It showed an enormous footprint and two tiny ones, all leading towards the carved bridge. Bigfoot loved it. He hadn't expected a present or even a thank-you when he had offered to help.

Evening · page 2
Target: audio/bedtime/bigfoot-page-09.mp3
Bigfoot's Evening — 2

He continued home, humming so softly that the woodland animals hardly noticed him. Somewhere in the distance, a hiker might have wondered about the huge footprints in the mud. But our story's Bigfoot was perfectly happy without being famous. Doing something kind had already made his day special.

On his way home, Bigfoot stopped beneath an enormous tree and admired the little picture on the leaf. The two small footprints looked rather funny next to the great big one. He traced the shape of the carved bridge with his finger. Nobody had watched his little adventure except Fern and her friend, and he was quite happy about that. A soft bird call came from the branches above, followed by the rustle of leaves. The forest was full of quiet things worth noticing, and Bigfoot liked being one of them.

Bigfoot's Evening — 3

The stream whispered beneath the evening sky. Bigfoot settled into his hidden forest home and placed the picture beside his bed of soft leaves. Kindness, he thought, didn't need an audience or a round of applause. It was enough to make somebody's journey a little easier. He closed his eyes and rested.
