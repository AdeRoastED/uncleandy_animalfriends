Sami's Morning — 1

Sami pushed his whiskery nose through an opening in the ice and took a splendid breath of cold Arctic air. “Pffff!” he blew, sending drops of water everywhere. His mother surfaced beside him. “Good morning, little fountain.” Sami grinned and disappeared beneath the water with a graceful twist.

Morning · page 2

Sami's Morning — 2

Under the ice, the world was blue and quiet. Sami's whiskers were wonderfully sensitive to movements in the water. He practised following the little swirls left by Mother as she swam. Then she led him back to their breathing opening. A ridge of snow stood beside it, shaped rather like a crooked white hat.

Sami turned beneath the opening and saw a pale patch of light shining through the water above him. He rose beside Mother, breathed, and went back down for another short exploration. The snowy ridge looked quite different from underwater, but the opening was easy to recognise when he was close to it. “A little window to the sky,” he thought. He liked the way his whiskers noticed movements he could not immediately see. But no matter how interesting the world beneath the ice became, he would always need to come up for air.

Sami's Morning — 3

“That's our window to the sky,” Sami decided. Seals are mammals, so even though they are excellent swimmers, they must return to air to breathe. Mother reminded him to stay close and surface regularly. They played near the opening, taking turns peeking above the water. Sami liked the way the sky appeared and disappeared.

Afternoon · page 1

Sami's Afternoon — 1

In the afternoon, a shiny fish flickered past Sami's nose. It turned left, then right, then vanished behind a dark patch of ice. Sami started to follow, imagining the most magnificent fish supper ever. He was so interested that he nearly forgot the little game he had played with Mother.

The fish flashed once more, and Sami imagined himself catching the most wonderful dinner. Then it disappeared into the darker water. Sami could still sense little movements around him, but he could no longer see the pale patch of light as clearly. Mother was close, turning towards the place where they had surfaced before. Sami's excitement began to give way to a much more ordinary feeling: he wanted a breath. The fish might be very interesting, but it was certainly not important enough to forget the window to the sky.

Sami's Afternoon — 2

He noticed Mother turning back towards their breathing place and remembered her lesson. The fish could wait. He followed her straight towards the familiar opening, where he surfaced and breathed comfortably. “There you are, sky!” he exclaimed. He rested for a while, watching snowflakes land on Mother's wet head and melt.

Afternoon · page 3
Target: audio/bedtime/seal-page-07.mp3
Sami's Afternoon — 3

When they dived again, Sami stayed near Mother and paid attention to his own need for air. This time he discovered several fish without chasing the first one far away. His whiskers helped him sense their movements. One fish darted behind him, and Sami spun so quickly that he almost chased his own flipper.

When Sami surfaced again, he felt the cool air touch his nose. He gave a little puff of satisfaction. The fish had been quick, the water had been interesting, and he had still managed to take the breaks he needed. Mother rested nearby while he looked down through the opening. A silver shape flickered far below, but this time Sami did not immediately dive after it. He could go back when he was ready. For now he watched a tiny snowflake land on his whisker and vanish.

Sami's Evening — 1

Later, Mother climbed onto a safe stretch of ice and Sami followed. Out of the water he was much less graceful, wriggling forward on his tummy. “I think someone has borrowed my beautiful swimming legs,” he joked. Mother settled beside him, and they watched the water move gently below.

Evening · page 2

Sami's Evening — 2

Sami thought about the shiny fish. He had wanted to catch it very badly, yet he had enjoyed the afternoon much more when he remembered his breathing breaks. “The fish isn't in charge of my day,” he decided. “My body gets a vote too.” Mother gave him a soft, approving nudge.

Out on the ice, Sami listened to the gentle sounds of the water beneath them. He stretched one flipper and then the other, feeling the pleasant tiredness of an afternoon spent swimming. He imagined the little fish telling its friends about a seal who had been very interested in supper but even more interested in breathing. “A remarkably sensible seal,” he decided. Mother gave a soft sound beside him. Sami liked that there would be another chance to explore later, without needing to turn every interesting discovery into a race.

Sami's Evening — 3

The evening sky grew darker above their resting place. Sami listened to the little splashes beneath the ice and felt his breathing become slow and easy. His next adventure could wait until another swim. He rested beside Mother, dreaming of a whole school of silver fish dancing under his window to the sky.
