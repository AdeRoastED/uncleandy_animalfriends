Audio assets for Uncle Andy Animal Friends

Included: original synthetic effects for five legends; two synthetic underwater ambience clips (turtle and octopus); two rendered music loops based on the app's existing melodies. These are not recordings of real animals.

Remaining: 23 real animal recordings, professional narration, facts and day stories recordings, optional songs. The app uses spoken imitations for missing real calls; it labels them. The app does not fetch external sound URLs at runtime.

To add reviewed genuine animal recordings, put a licensed MP3 in sounds/{animal}.mp3 and add its ID to VERIFIED_CALLS in index.html. Keep a source, author, licence and edit record in extras/animal_sounds_list.csv.

Narration paths:
stories/{animal}-morning.mp3 (also -afternoon, -evening)
facts/{animal}-1.mp3, -2.mp3 ...
bedtime/page-01.mp3 (shared Uncle Andy welcome)
bedtime/page-02.mp3 ... page-10.mp3 (Sunny)
bedtime/{animal}-page-02.mp3 ... page-10.mp3 (other stories)
songs/{animal}.mp3 (optional)
music/theme.mp3 and music/lullaby.mp3 (included)
Full narration script: extras/elevenlabs_script.csv
